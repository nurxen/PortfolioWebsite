Common questions

FAQ

What kind of projects do you work on? +

Primarily 3D environment and character assets for games (Unity/Unreal) and cinematic projects. I'm comfortable with everything from a single hero prop to a full modular environment kit with lighting, shaders and engine integration.

Are you available for freelance or studio roles? +

Yes — I'm open to junior/mid 3D Artist or Technical Artist roles as well as freelance collaborations on game jams, indie projects or short-term commissions. Based in Madrid, remote-friendly worldwide.

What does your delivery look like? +

Assets arrive engine-ready: organized prefabs, clean naming conventions, documented UV and texture sets, and a quick integration walkthrough if needed. I use GitHub for version control so collaboration with dev teams is seamless.

Do you work in styles other than cozy/stylized? +

Absolutely. My personal aesthetic leans cozy, but UNCHAINED is a psychological horror game — so I'm equally comfortable with dark, gritty or realistic pipelines. Style follows the project's brief.
