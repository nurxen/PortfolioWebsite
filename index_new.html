Who I am

Where art and
code meet

I'm an Environment Artist with a technical background in game development. My work focuses on building high-quality, real-time-ready assets from concept to engine — handling the full pipeline: modelling, UV layout, PBR baking, Shader Graph and baked lighting inside Unity 6.

I enjoy sitting at the intersection of art and programming teams — communicating technical constraints in a language artists understand, and artistic intent in a language engineers can implement. Currently studying at Universidad Rey Juan Carlos, with an Erasmus stint at Haaga-Helia (Helsinki).
