I am an Environment Artist with a solid technical background in Game Development. My focus is on creating high-quality assets that are fully optimized for real-time rendering.

I specialize in end-to-end integration within Unity. Using Git-based version control, I manage the assembly of modular environments, prefab systems, and animation state machines. My goal is to deliver art that is not only visually compelling but also technically seamless for the programming team.

Technical Expertise:

  • ✦ Unity Integration: Prefab systems & lighting.
  • ✦ Version Control: Professional Git/GitHub workflow.
  • ✦ Asset Pipeline: High-to-low poly baking & PBR.
  • ✦ Tech Art: Custom shaders & performance optimization.
