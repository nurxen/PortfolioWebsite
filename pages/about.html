About Me

I've always been fascinated by the intersection of storytelling and interactive media. My journey into 3D art is driven by a passion for building immersive worlds—from the initial creative concept to the complex systems that bring them to life.

With a hybrid background in Game Development and Technical Art, I bridge the gap between artistic vision and engineering performance. I manage the full 3D pipeline, specializing in PBR modeling, shaders, and lighting, while constantly exploring AI-driven workflows to boost production efficiency without sacrificing quality.

I thrive in agile, collaborative environments where clear communication is key. My goal is to push the boundaries of real-time environments and contribute my technical and creative expertise to high-standard, impactful projects.
