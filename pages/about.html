My Specialization: Professional Simulation & Game Development

I'm a Technical Artist with deep expertise in real-time simulation for professional and educational applications. My journey combines commercial game development with specialized experience in clinical simulation environments—where performance, precision, and scalability are non-negotiable.

My flagship project is the ECOE Medical Simulator (TFG, published on Steam), a clinical training application I built from the ground up. This experience taught me the demands of professional-grade simulation: modular pipelines, aggressive optimization, and working within strict performance constraints. Every draw call, every shader, every texture atlas matters.

Beyond simulation, I'm an active game developer. UNCHAINED (psychological horror, Steam) evolved from winning the Game Scholars Jam—my first venture into commercial game development. I also contribute to game jams regularly, consistently placing (Crosspaws 3rd Place, Once Button a Time 3rd Place, Ant&Estres 2nd Place), which keeps me sharp on rapid prototyping and creative problem-solving.

Technical Philosophy: Form Follows Function

I believe that artistic beauty and technical efficiency must coexist. A stunning environment that crushes frame rate is a failed environment. My approach:

  • Modular-First Architecture: Design for reusability, scalability, and optimization from day one.
  • Optimization as Design: Channel Packing, texture atlasing, draw call reduction—these aren't afterthoughts, they're core design decisions.
  • Shader Development: Custom node graphs for procedural materials and visual coherence without bloat.
  • Performance Profiling: Understanding CPU vs GPU bottlenecks, memory footprint, and real-world constraints.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Speak the language of programmers, designers, and producers. No silos.
