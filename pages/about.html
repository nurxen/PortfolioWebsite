My Story & Passion

I've always been fascinated by the intersection of storytelling and interactive media. My journey into 3D art is driven by a deep passion for building immersive worlds—from the initial creative concept and lookdev to the complex technical systems that bring them to life seamlessly on screen.

Having a hybrid background in Game Development and Technical Art allows me to speak both languages fluently. I understand how an artist wants a scene to feel, but I also know how the engine calculates draw calls, memory limits, and shader instructions. This balance ensures that beauty never compromises performance.

Whether I am assembling modular environment architectures, configuring dynamic lighting setups, or optimizing channel-packed textures, my goal is always to empower the rest of the team with clean, reliable assets.

How I Work & Collaborate

I thrive in structured, highly collaborative studio environments. I believe that great games and simulations are built on clear communication, proactive problem-solving, and continuous feedback loops.

I am organized, reliable, and comfortable adapting to pipeline constraints. I don't just deliver models; I investigate solutions, anticipate engine limitations, and make sure every asset fits its purpose within the broader scope of the project.
